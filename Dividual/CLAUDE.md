## 1 · What this is

**Dividual** is an independent retention-diagnostics practice for live-service games. It tells a studio where its players are actually leaving, which of its assumed causes survive a validation gate, and what to instrument next — then builds the instrument.

The offer: a fixed-price **diagnostic** that scopes and justifies a value-priced **build**. Give away the reframe; charge for the construction.

Everything in this repo serves one goal: **new business.**

## 2 · The boundary, in one line

Show the **diagnosis** — the reframe, the existence claim, the *result* of a gate ("the real cause held, the decoy failed"). Withhold the **prescription's construction** — the discriminator, the estimable-object build, the fitted instrument.

Full tiering in `00_START/BOUNDARY-RULES.md`. It governs. Two rules bear repeating because they are the easiest to breach by accident:

- **No client names, client numbers, or client telemetry without written permission** in any *public or prospect-facing* artifact. The BO7 study is client-specific; the site and outreach materials say **"a live-service publisher."**

## 3 · ⚠ Evidence discipline — added 31 July 2026, non-negotiable

A forensic audit of ~24,000 archive documents found that several long-standing claims in Adnan's professional materials originated in **machine-generated summary files from mid-2025** and had propagated, unchecked, into a dozen live documents. Some were also on this site's source material.

**The governing rule: a document asserting a fact is not evidence of that fact.** Grade by document type:

| Grade | What it is | Usable? |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Independently verifiable outside our control — DOI, IRS 990, state registry, publisher record, a third party's own site | Yes, alone |
| **B** | Primary artifact with embedded metadata — the deliverable itself, `dc:creator`, git history, data pulled from a binary | Yes, alone |
| **C** | Contemporaneous third-party document — a counterparty's signed contract, an invoice, an email *from* someone else | Yes, alone |
| **D** | Contemporaneous self-authored | Corroborates only |
| **E** | Retrospective self-authored summary | **No** |
| **F** | Machine-generated | **No** |

Rarity is **not** evidence against. A single occurrence is judged by its document type, never by frequency. Conversely, fifteen documents repeating a claim is one source if they derive from each other — trace to the earliest and grade that.

## 5 · Voice

Concrete before abstract. A scene, a number, or a mechanism opens; the concept arrives to name what the reader already recognizes. Rhythm varies — short sentences against long ones. Endings hold tension rather than resolving into uplift.

Hard prohibitions in all prose: **no negation-pivot** ("not X, it is Y" / "rather than" / "instead of" in any punctuation — use two positive statements in sequence); no significance-stamps ("that is the point," "herein lies"); no participial-flourish fragments. Banned words: delve, navigate, landscape, lens, underscore, highlight, showcase, leverage, robust *(except "doubly-robust," the estimator)*, nuanced, multifaceted, intricate, tapestry, testament to, meticulous, seamless, holistic, paradigm, ecosystem, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art.

**Watch "churn."** It is the industry term and it turns into a tic fast. Two or three uses per document is the ceiling; otherwise: departures, exits, going quiet, the cliff, the fade, lapse, drop-off.

Run the de-AI scan before anything ships. Zero high-severity is the bar.

## 6 · Working practice

- **Small named units.** State the unit and its one governing constraint before drafting.
- **Honesty over sycophancy.** Challenge weak reasoning, including Adnan's and your own. "Not found" and "can't identify that" are valuable results. Rigor is the respect.
- **Keep the corrective loop running.** After building anything, pressure-test it: where would a hostile quant attack this, where is it weak, what did you assume.
- **Never present synthetic results as findings.** Every plot from `30_tools/` is watermarked *"illustrative synthetic data — not a finding."* Do not add a save path that bypasses `plots.finalize()`.
- **Flag rather than silently rewrite** anything touching Adnan's voice or his credentials.
