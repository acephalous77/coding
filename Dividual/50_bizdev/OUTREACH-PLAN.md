# Cold-approach plan — gaming UR / player-insights stakeholders

A plan to build a pipeline of studios and stakeholders who would consider the framework for their own churn, engagement, and player-wellbeing work. Draft with recommendations; the strategic dials at the end are yours to set before we execute.

## 0. The one hard rule — the confidentiality firewall

The Activision / BO7 study, its data, and its specific numbers do not leave the ABK context. Nothing from that engagement goes into any outreach — no figures, no findings, no "here's what I found at Activision." At most, a single sanitized line: *"I've applied this to AAA live-service multiplayer churn telemetry."* Everything else is the framework and the method, which are yours.

Consequence: before serious outreach, build **one non-ABK proof artifact** — the same first-passage / gates-first method run on a public or synthetic game-telemetry dataset, producing a small shareable study. That becomes the thing you can actually show. It is the highest-leverage item on the list, and I can build it with you.

## 1. What you're offering, and why you

**The wedge — a diagnostic engagement.** Lead with a low-friction offer, not a framework lecture: *"Give me a slice of your churn or engagement data under NDA, and I'll tell you what your current metrics can't see — where your churn actually is, which of your assumptions the data won't support, and what to instrument next."* It converts to a bigger engagement or a role, and it is easy to say yes to.

**The differentiator — hold these four, they are unusual together:**
- **Gates-first rigor.** You kill your own hypotheses before believing them. A UR lab that has been burned by confident dashboards will recognize this instantly.
- **Theory-to-practice bridge.** Critical theory of engagement plus real telemetry — you name the structure and then measure it. Few people do both.
- **The humanist ethic.** Player satisfaction over extraction, operationalized (satiation vs satisfaction, the exit option). This is a live and regulation-timely concern for responsible-design and wellbeing teams.
- **Cross-industry vision.** Games, streaming, and gambling as one engagement lineage — you see the pattern the in-house team can't, because they live in one product.

Your profile (PhD critical theory, humanitarian logistics, teaching, industry-adjacent research) is the hook when framed as range, not as a detour.

## 3. Value proposition, calibrated per stakeholder

- **UR / player-insights managers:** a rigorous, additive diagnostic lens and a collaborator who bridges the qual and the quant; someone who makes their team's read sharper, not someone selling a black box.
- **Data science / ML leads:** the first-passage and regime framing, and the gates-first, case-control, leakage discipline that catches the traps their own extracts hide (the recency-confound story lands hard here).
- **Live-ops / design:** the intervention logic — churn as an abrupt first passage means act early; the two-tier targeting; focus-over-variety onboarding.
- **Responsible-design / wellbeing:** the satisfaction-versus-capture ethic made measurable — a way to tell a satiated-and-leaving player from a captured one, which is exactly the distinction regulators and wellbeing teams are reaching for.

## 4. Channels and sequencing — warm first, always

1. **Warm intros (convert far better than cold).** Map your network first: the GUR community, any ex-Ubisoft Massive contacts, academic colleagues now in industry, and — once the ABK engagement is on solid footing — Ahmad himself as a reference. A one-line intro from a peer beats twenty cold notes.
2. **Publish to pull inbound and warm the cold.** A sanitized methodology piece (the Loot Loop Substack fits), the public-data proof study, and a conference or GRUX talk. Thought-leadership means your cold note lands on someone who has already seen your thinking.
3. **Tiered cold, insight-led.** LinkedIn connect with a specific note, then a short email. The opener leads with an **insight about their game or their problem**, not a pitch — a public observation about their churn or live-service challenge, read through the lens, ending in a low-friction ask (a 20-minute call, or "want the one-pager?"). Generic outreach dies; specific outreach gets replies.

## 6. Message skeletons (structure only; full drafts once you pick targets)

- **LinkedIn connect (≤300 chars):** one specific line about their game/problem + one line on your angle + no ask. Just connect.
- **Cold email (≤150 words):** (a) a sharp, specific insight about their churn/engagement problem; (b) one line on the method that produces it (gates-first, first-passage, the confound); (c) the sanitized credibility line; (d) a single low-friction CTA. No attachment on the first touch.
- **Warm-intro request (to a mutual):** two sentences the connector can forward verbatim — who you are, the one specific reason this person would care.
