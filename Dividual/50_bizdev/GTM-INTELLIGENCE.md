# Go-to-market intelligence — the churn/engagement diagnostic, NA

Deep-research synthesis, five verified streams, prioritized 2024–2026 sources. Confidence is flagged per claim (High / Medium / Low). Where the evidence cuts against the hopeful reading, it says so — the honest reads are the load-bearing ones.

## Executive summary — the five things that matter

1. **The white space is real and specific.** The field splits into three lanes that don't touch each other's problem — qualitative UX/playtesting, behavioral-analytics SaaS, and market/motivation intelligence — and **no one sells the join**: statistical identification of *why* a player leaves (satiation vs friction vs a genuine satisfaction ceiling), carried by an engagement-ethics stance, translated from theory into a studio's Monday decisions. The two anchor independents bracket the gap without filling it: **Anders Drachen** owns academic quant credibility with no ethics frame; **Celia Hodent** owns design-ethics credibility with no statistical/churn frame. *(High.)*
2. **The ethics angle has its strongest *paying* buyer in regulated sectors, not games.** In games UR, "satisfaction vs manipulation" measurement is not yet an established external-advisory category — current spend is toxicity, child-safety, age-assurance. In **online gambling/sports-betting**, the same distinction has a **statutory buyer**: affordability checks, "markers of harm," the SAFE Bet Act, and state RG rules force operators to *measure* engagement-harm defensibly. That is where the satiation-vs-satisfaction framing is a compliance line item, not a nice-to-have. *(Medium-High.)*
3. **The layoff "tailwind" is unproven and may cut both ways.** 2024 was the worst year on record for games layoffs (~14,600–15,000+; ~45,000 cumulative 2022–mid-2025; GDC 2026: 33% of US games workers laid off in two years). But there is **no UR-specific layoff count**, and general UX-research postings fell ~89% from their 2022 peak — overall research demand contracted too. Do not lead with "layoffs opened room for consultants"; it is a hypothesis, and budgets for *external* help may have shrunk alongside headcount. *(High confidence in the caveat.)*
4. **The regulatory forcing function is the real demand engine.** FTC dark-patterns enforcement (Epic $245M; HoYoverse/Genshin $20M loot-box "dark patterns," Jan 2025), the EU Digital Fairness Act (addictive-design proposal ~2026), Ofcom's extraterritorial gaming guidance, and the SAFE Bet Act's affordability mandate all convert "engagement design" into something studios and operators must be able to *defend with measurement*. Regulators are converging on **disclosure and dark-patterns** (measurable deception), which is friendlier to a measurement-and-identification framing than to a moralized one. *(High.)*
5. **The community is small, interconnected, and has an ABK node at its center.** The IGDA Games Research & User Experience (GRUX) SIG is the field's hub, and its **Chair, Hannah Murphy, is a Senior User Researcher at Activision** (ex-Xbox, ex-Epic). That is the single highest-value warm path found — and a reason to run competitor outreach through the *community*, not around it. *(High.)*

**The four-dial recommendation, up front:** (a) **parallel, community-first** — keep ABK primary, build presence through GRUX and thought-leadership, and don't aggressively cold-pitch direct ABK competitors for consulting while courting a role there (the world is too small); (b) **consulting-diagnostic as the lead**, employment an acceptable parallel outcome, licensing premature; (c) **games-first for credibility, responsible-gambling as the deliberate second beachhead** where the ethics framing has a statutory buyer; (d) **build the public brand now** — nobody owns "satiation vs satisfaction" publicly, and occupying it is the cheapest way to warm everything, ABK kept confidential.

## Part 1 — Market & demand: the games UR function

**How it's structured.** No single standard owner of player insight exists across studios; analytics and qualitative UR are often in different departments. Riot's combined ~70-person "Insights" org (user research + market research + analytics, embedded/matrix) is the most-cited *exception* and reference model. *(Medium.)* In live-service studios, churn/retention/lifecycle analytics usually sits in a data-science/BI function organized around acquisition–retention–monetization, distinct from the qualitative UR/playtesting group. *(Medium.)* In-house UX-research groups exist at essentially every major publisher (EA, PlayStation, Ubisoft, King, Zynga, Riot, Microsoft, Scopely). *(High.)*

**The layoffs.** Worst year on record in 2024; GDC 2025 survey: 11% laid off in the prior year (narrative hardest at 19%, business/finance least at 6%; UR not a broken-out category); GDC 2026: 33% of US workers laid off over two years, ~two-thirds of AAA respondents at studios that cut. Named, dated cuts: Unity ~1,800 (25%, Jan 2024); Epic ~830 (16%, Sep 2023); EA ~670 (5%, Feb 2024); Bungie 220 (17%, Jul 2024, now folding into PlayStation); Microsoft ~9,000 company-wide (Jul 2025, Xbox hard-hit). *(High.)*

**The honest read.** There is a real external games-UR/playtesting market (PlaytestCloud ran ~5,400 playtests in 2024, ~$34M est. revenue), and consultancies market on scarce senior in-house capacity. But the layoffs→external-demand causal link is **under-evidenced**, and the ~89% drop in UX-research postings suggests total research demand fell. The defensible pitch is the **regulatory forcing function** (below), not the layoff wave.

## Part 1b — Wellbeing / responsible design / regulation (the demand engine)

Responsible design is now a staffed function: Riot's cross-studio "Player Dynamics" discipline; EA's "Positive Play" + its first Transparency Report (2024) + FC Playtime limits; Xbox's AI safety operations; Activision/Call of Duty's third-party voice moderation (Modulate ToxMod, ~43% drop in exposure to disruptive voice chat, Oct 2024); Roblox's trust-and-safety at ~10% of staff. *(High.)*

Regulatory drivers, dated: FTC/Epic $245M dark-patterns order ($126M refunds sent Jun 2025); **FTC/HoYoverse $20M, Jan 17 2025 — loot boxes named "dark-pattern tactics," odds disclosure, under-16 ban** (High, primary); ESRB random-items label since 2020; California AADC (effective Jul 2024, litigation ongoing, "well-being" language struck as vague); EU Digital Fairness Fitness Check (Oct 2024, addictive-design case study) → **Digital Fairness Act proposal ~2026**; European Parliament resolution on online-game consumer protection (Jan 2023, 577 votes); UK Online Safety Act + Ofcom gaming guidance (Oct 2025, extraterritorial to US studios serving UK). *(High.)*

**The nuance that shapes positioning:** current demand is concentrated in trust-and-safety/toxicity/child-safety/age-assurance; a market for "is our engagement satisfying or manipulative" measurement is **not yet established**. Validated academic instruments exist (PENS, BANGS 2024, GUESS), but not as a commercial advisory category. So this is a **forcing-function / near-future play**: the regulatory line is moving toward measurable dark-patterns and disclosure, and the satiation-vs-satisfaction distinction is the measurement that gets ahead of it.

## Part 2 — Engagement-adjacent: streaming vs gambling

**Streaming buys retention as pure commercial optimization.** Netflix frames engagement ("time spent") as its "best proxy" for satisfaction, which "leads to higher retention"; ties pricing to churn/retention signals; claims "industry-leading retention" (its churn numbers are third-party estimates, not disclosed — do not quote the 2.17% as Netflix's). Spotify credits features with cutting churn and raised prices across 150+ markets without material churn. McKinsey and Deloitte sell subscription-retention advisory as named practices. *(High/Medium.)*

**Gambling buys retention under a regulatory obligation — the ethics wedge.** DraftKings and FanDuel name engagement/retention as core value drivers (FanDuel US Average Monthly Players +15% YoY, Q4 2024); BetMGM attributes retention to analytics-driven CRM. The difference that matters: the **SAFE Bet Act** (reintroduced Mar 2025) would mandate affordability checks; multiple states already require **data-driven/algorithmic problem-gambling intervention**; and there is an active peer-reviewed science of measuring gambling engagement-harm from account data ("markers of harm," 2024 replication; a 2025 scoping review on data-science risk-assessment models). This is the one place the satiation-vs-satisfaction distinction has an **external, statutory buyer**, not just an internal one. *(Medium-High.)*

**The method transfers.** Survival/hazard analysis, LTV, and ensemble churn models appear in identical form across games, streaming, gambling, and season-ticket churn. First-passage/hazard framing is portable at the toolkit level; the ethics framing is what differentiates it in the regulated sectors.

## Part 3 — Competitive landscape and the white space

**The three lanes (none fills the join):**
- **Qualitative UX / playtesting:** Player Research (Keywords), PlaytestCloud, plus independents Steve Bromley (ex-PlayStation), Celia Hodent (ex-Epic; ethics-forward but not statistical). Answers "do players understand/enjoy this build," pre-launch, small-N, no hazard modeling.
- **Behavioral-analytics SaaS:** GameAnalytics, Unity/deltaDNA, Amplitude, **Solsten** (psychographic, $22M Konvoy round 2022; "player personas"). Answers "who is about to churn — suppress it." Retention is the objective; satiation and satisfaction are collapsed into one "engaged" signal.
- **Market/motivation intelligence:** GameRefinery (Liftoff) — 12 motivational drivers, market-level, not individual first-passage.

**The anchor independents to position between:** **Anders Drachen** (Prof., York; *Game Analytics*, *Game Data Science*) = academic quant, no ethics frame. **Celia Hodent** (*The Gamer's Brain*, Ethical Games) = design ethics, no churn/stats frame. **Mike Ambinder** (ex-Valve, Cognitive Explorations) = hard-science experimental. None sells statistical churn-identification fused with engagement ethics and theory-to-practice translation. **That triple is the defensible white space**, and the 2024–2025 FTC wave is its tailwind: studios now have a compliance-and-reputation reason to want a churn diagnostic that is defensibly *not* dark-pattern retention. *(Medium-High.)*

**Pricing reference (what's public):** Games-specific consultant day rates are **not published anywhere** found. General-UX proxies: freelance UX researcher ~$60–80/hr, day rate ~$560–730; senior/games specialists at or above the top. Vendor bands for context: PlaytestCloud €1,130–2,825/mo; GameAnalytics free → $499+/mo (Slack channel above $4k CV); Amplitude free → $49/mo → custom (~$500–$10k+/mo est.). Implication: an independent expert diagnostic prices on value, not tooling; a defensible band is a fixed-scope diagnostic project (a few weeks) in the low-to-mid five figures, with day-rate framing kept private until a benchmark is confirmed by outreach.

## Part 5 — Strategy synthesis

**Positioning (one line):** the independent who identifies *why* players leave — satiation versus friction versus a real satisfaction ceiling — with statistical rigor and an engagement-ethics stance, and translates it into what a studio does Monday. You are the join between Drachen's rigor and Hodent's ethics that nobody currently sells.

**Lead offer — the NDA diagnostic.** A fixed-scope, few-week read on a slice of a client's churn/engagement data: where their churn actually is, which assumptions the data won't support, and what to instrument next. Low-friction to say yes to; converts to a larger engagement or a role. Price on value (low-to-mid five figures per diagnostic), day rate held private until benchmarked.

**Proof asset (do this first, non-negotiable):** the non-ABK proof study on public or synthetic telemetry — the only thing you can show. Nothing else is safe, and it de-risks every conversation.

**Channel mix, in order of yield:** (1) the GRUX community and GamesUR Summit — warm, and where the freelance-GUR path is normalized (Sebastian Long is the template); (2) thought-leadership — the Loot Loop Substack, a methodology piece, a public-data mini-study — to occupy the white space and warm cold contacts; (3) tiered, insight-led outreach to the named leaders above, and to the regulated-sector buyers (gambling RG-analytics, where the ethics framing has a statutory home).

**90-day sequence:** Weeks 1–2 — build the proof study, the one-pager, the LinkedIn positioning. Weeks 3–6 — enter the GRUX community, publish the methodology piece, request warm intros (Bromley/Long as community entry; Hannah Murphy handled per the note below). Weeks 6–12 — insight-led outreach to 2–3 named studio leaders and 2–3 regulated-sector buyers; book the first diagnostic.

## Part 6 — The four dials, with the evidence behind each

- **(a) Parallel vs hold → parallel, community-first, ABK-primary.** The games-UR world is small and interconnected, and its community Chair sits inside Activision. Aggressively cold-pitching direct ABK competitors (Riot, EA, PlayStation, Xbox) for consulting while you court an ABK role could travel back through that network. Run the parallel track through the *community* (GRUX, publishing) and through *non-ABK-competitor* sectors (gambling, streaming) — both raise your value without creating a divided-loyalty optic. Keep ABK the anchor.
- **(b) Consulting vs job vs licensing → consulting-diagnostic lead; employment an acceptable parallel; licensing later.** No external-advisory category exists yet for the ethics-measurement join, so licensing has no established buyers — premature. The diagnostic wedge converts to either a contract or a role, and given the tight post-layoff market a UR-lab role (Fisher's or Pagulayan's world) is a legitimate destination, not a fallback.
- **(c) Games-first vs broader → games-first for credibility, responsible-gambling as the deliberate second beachhead.** Games is your network and the ABK reference. But the ethics framing's strongest *paying, regulated* demand is in gambling (SAFE Bet Act, markers-of-harm, state RG mandates). Open that second front early — it's where "satiation vs satisfaction" is a compliance requirement, not a philosophy.
- **(d) Public brand → build it now, ABK confidential.** Nobody owns the satiation-vs-satisfaction measurement position publicly; it is the cheapest white space to occupy, and it warms every cold approach. Publish the framework and method, never the engagement.
