# Disclosure log

*PRIVATE. Standard professional practice for pre-contract technical conversations: a dated record of what was shown or said, to whom, at what depth. Neutral by design — this protects everyone's recollection, including theirs. Fill the meeting row the same day, from the debrief.*

## Depth codes

- **E** — existence claim only (the thing exists; the how withheld)
- **M** — mechanism / published-method level
- **F** — form of a deliverable (synthetic exhibit; construction withheld)
- **C** — construction / discriminator *(should never appear in a pre-contract row)*

## Standing rules for every row

1. If a construction was accidentally reached in conversation, log it honestly the same day — the log's value is its candor.
2. Whatever is handed over gets listed by filename. Nothing else counts as "shared."
3. The log is private working record; it is never itself shared.
