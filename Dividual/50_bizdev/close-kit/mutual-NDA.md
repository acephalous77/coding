# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — template

> **⚠ Template, not legal advice.** I'm not a lawyer, and this is a starting scaffold. Have counsel review and adapt it before you send or sign anything. Fill every `[bracket]`.

**Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement**

This Agreement is entered into as of **[effective date]** between **[Dividual legal entity, form, address]** ("Dividual") and **[Counterparty legal name, form, address]** ("Counterparty"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

**Purpose.** To evaluate, scope, and — if the Parties agree — undertake a player-churn diagnostic engagement (the "Purpose").

1. **Confidential Information.** Non-public information disclosed by either Party, marked confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances — including, without limitation, Counterparty's telemetry, player data, and business information, and Dividual's frameworks, methods, models, and materials.

2. **Exclusions.** Information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party without obligation before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice where legally permitted.

3. **Use and care.** The receiving Party will use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose, protect it with at least the care it uses for its own like information (and no less than reasonable care), and disclose it only to personnel and advisors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these.

4. **Term and survival.** Confidentiality obligations run for **[3]** years from the date of disclosure; information that qualifies as a trade secret remains protected for as long as it so qualifies under applicable law.

5. **Return or destruction.** On written request, the receiving Party will return or destroy Confidential Information, subject to routine archival backups and legal-retention requirements.

6. **No license; no obligation.** Nothing here transfers ownership or grants a license, and neither Party is obligated to proceed with any engagement.

7. **Dividual background IP.** Dividual's pre-existing frameworks, methods, models, and know-how remain Dividual's sole property; this Agreement grants Counterparty no right, title, license, or interest in them. Deliverables produced for Counterparty are governed by a separate engagement agreement.

8. **No warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is," without warranty.

9. **Miscellaneous.** Governing law: **[state/jurisdiction]**. This Agreement is the entire understanding on its subject and may be signed in counterparts.

**[Dividual]** — signature, name, title, date
**[Counterparty]** — signature, name, title, date

---

*Clause 7 (background-IP carve-out) is load-bearing: it keeps the method Dividual's while the engagement gives the client the findings and, under the build, the fitted instrument. Keep it in every version.*
