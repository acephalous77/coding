# The Diagnostic — scope & deliverables

*Dividual · dividual.studio · [hello@dividual.studio]*

A fixed-scope read on a slice of your player telemetry that tells you where your churn actually is, which of your assumed causes is real, and the one thing worth instrumenting next. Plain-language first; the technical apparatus runs underneath.

## What you get

- A short, plain-language report — the findings, no wall of jargon.
- A 45-minute readout session with your team.
- A pre-registered experiment design to confirm the cause on your own players.

## What it tells you

- **Where your churn sits** — its shape (abrupt exit versus gradual fade) as well as its rate.
- **Which of your assumed causes survive a validation gate**, and which fail — the real driver accepted, the plausible decoy rejected.
- **The confounds your data won't support** — the questions it can't answer honestly, named up front so you don't chase them.
- **The one thing worth instrumenting next.**

## What you do Monday

Instrument that one thing, and run the experiment we pre-register with you.

## Scope & timeline

- A defined slice of telemetry (a sample cohort — see the data request).
- 3–4 weeks, one mid-point check-in, a fixed fee.

## Out of scope for the diagnostic (available as the follow-on build)

The fitted early-warning instrument, productionization, ongoing monitoring, and the causal experiments run to completion. The diagnostic scopes and prices these; it doesn't deliver them.

## Your effort

About an hour to export a data slice against a schema we provide, plus the readout session.
