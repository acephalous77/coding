# How I work, and what it costs

*Dividual · dividual.studio · [hello@dividual.studio]*

Transparent by default — the workflow and the ranges are written down because the honesty is part of the offer.

## How I work — no surprises

1. **Intro call** (free, 30 min) — scope the problem, decide fit. No obligation.
2. **Scope + mutual NDA** (same day) — fixed scope, fixed price, fixed timeline, in writing.
3. **Data handoff** — a small, defined schema; about an hour of your team's time.
4. **The work** (fixed timeline) — one mid-point check-in; no black-box weeks.
5. **Readout** — a short report and a 45-minute session: the findings and one prioritized next step.
6. **Optional continuation** — the build or an advisory retainer, pre-quoted, so the next step is a decision.
